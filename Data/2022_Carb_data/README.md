# 2022 Carbohydrate Data

These are the 2022 LD11 leaf and stem carbohydrate measurement results.

GFS: the sum of glucose, fructose, sucrose concentrations (mg/g)

Starch: starch concentration (mg/g)

4 replicates from distinct plants are taken at each time point. 

Each replicate was measured twice. If the CV for the two measurements has a CV greater than 10%, the result was marked 0 in the "Valid_n" column and discarded.
